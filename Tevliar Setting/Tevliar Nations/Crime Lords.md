### Flak Fishwater
An elder Aboleth that has existed longer than most sentient species. Flak rules over their portion of Ragas from the ports, holding steadfast claim over the harbors and docks along the shore. Those who claim loyalty to Flak dedicate not just their bodies but their minds, forming a network of interconnected warlocks that exist in a hive mind hierarchy, the highest being physically twisted by the ancient power. 

Being ancient comes with many benefits, they hold knowledge that rivals entire libraries of text from the most ancient kingdoms. This knowledge as allowed them to construct a robust and hidden Edge dust production line deep in one of the bay trenches, only accessible by whomever Flak allows.
